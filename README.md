# BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis

##  Project Overview
This project was completed as part of my internship at **Sapphire IQ** in the **Retail & Grocery Sales Analytics** domain.  
The goal is to analyze BlinkIT’s grocery sales data to provide management with actionable insights into product performance, outlet efficiency, and customer behavior.

---

##  Objectives
### Objective 3: Find Out What Is Driving Better Sales Performance
- **Task 3.1:** Identify which types of products are selling more.  
- **Task 3.2:** Find out which types of outlets are performing better.  
- **Task 3.3:** Explore whether product visibility, weight, ratings, and outlet characteristics are connected with sales.  
- **Task 3.4:** Compare better-performing and lower-performing products and outlets.

### Objective 4: Help Management Make Better Business Decisions
- **Task 4.1:** Identify products contributing more to overall sales.  
- **Task 4.2:** Identify outlets and locations performing better.  
- **Task 4.3:** Highlight products, outlets, and areas where performance can be improved.  
- **Task 4.4:** Provide practical suggestions to improve sales and business performance.

---

##  Tools & Technologies
- **Python** (Jupyter Notebook)  
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
- **Dataset:** BlinkIT Grocery  Data (Excel format)

---

##  Key Insights
- Top product categories and outlet types driving sales.  
- Correlation between product visibility, weight, ratings, and sales.  
- Identification of underperforming outlets and improvement areas.  
- Practical recommendations for boosting sales and optimizing performance.

---

## Visualizations
- Bar charts for product and outlet sales.
